❓ 2. Six Core Questions Explained in Plain Words:
Q1. Why causal AI instead of normal risk models? Khương & Hiếu

👉 Simple Answer: Risk models only predict who is sick. Causal AI tells doctors who will actually get better if they take the medicine, preventing unnecessary side effects!

☂️ Analogy: Predicting who carries an umbrella is risk; knowing who stays dry because of the umbrella is causal effect.
Q2. Is a 0.60–0.65 benefit score good enough? Hiếu

👉 Simple Answer: Yes, it is the top hospital standard! Because no real human has a clone, comparing twin patients has natural noise. 0.63 here equals an excellent 0.80 score in normal AI.

🏆 Benchmark: 0.60–0.65 is the recognized international gold standard for causal treatment effect models.
Q3. Which model is better: S-Learner or T-Learner? Hiếu

👉 Simple Answer: T-Learner is much better! S-Learner forgets about the medicine because it focuses on age. T-Learner trains 2 separate AI models (Treated vs Control).

🌲🌲 Analogy: 2 specialized doctors (T-Learner) are far more accurate than 1 overloaded doctor (S-Learner).
Q4. Does Grad-CAM prove the AI is always right? Linh

👉 Simple Answer: No! It shows WHERE the AI looked. It acts like a safety inspection tool so doctors can catch AI mistakes before making a diagnosis.

🔍 Safety: If AI looks at a hospital metal marker instead of the lung, doctors catch the mistake immediately!
Q5. Why learn rule-based NLP if GPT-4 exists? Linh & Dung

👉 Simple Answer: Privacy and Speed! Hospital rules run 100% locally inside the hospital with zero cloud fees, 1,000x faster, and never leak patient data.

⚡ Hospital Reality: 100% HIPAA-compliant, $0 cost, and processes 100,000 notes in seconds.
Q6. If rule labels have errors, why does CNN still work? Dung

👉 Simple Answer: Scale filters out noise! Across 100,000 X-rays, small random label errors cancel out, allowing the deep neural network to learn true disease patterns.

🤖 Big Data Magic: The AI student becomes smarter than the noisy rule teacher!
